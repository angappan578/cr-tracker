CR Group Tracker – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026 (shared member list)

CR Group Tracker ("the app") is an Android app in which a group of users keeps one shared list of group members. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used and how you can remove it.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: your username, and optionally your e-mail address. Passwords are never stored as plain text; only a salted hash is kept.
  • Sign in with Google: if you choose it, we receive your Google account e-mail address and Google account ID from Google. We do not receive your Google password and we do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.
  • Member data you enter: member names, contact numbers, joining dates, manager/parent member and level information, and (entered by admins only) whether the member has withdrawn the joining deposit. The deposit status is visible to admins only.
  • Technical data: login session tokens (stored only as hashes) and an activity log of changes made to your member list (for example "member added"), used for security and troubleshooting.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising ID or any device data, and it contains no ads or third-party analytics.

2. How we use the data

  • To create your account and sign you in.
  • To store the shared member list and show it to every user of the app, together with the username of the user who added each member.
  • To send a password-reset code to your e-mail address when you ask for one.

We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone for advertising or marketing.

3. Where the data is stored

Data is stored in Google Sheets in a Google account controlled by the app administrator, accessed through Google Apps Script, and is subject to Google's privacy policy.

The member list is shared: every member you add (name, contact number, joining date, manager and level) is visible to all users of the app, along with your username as the person who added it. Only admins can add, edit or delete members; other users can view the list and send a request to the admins to add a member (the admins see your username with the request). Your password, e-mail address and Google account details are never shown to other users. Do not add a person's details unless they agree to be listed.

A copy of the member list is cached on your phone so the app works offline. All data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections.

4. Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. To delete your account and all of your member data, e-mail us at the address below from the e-mail linked to your account (or tell us your username). We will delete it within 30 days. Uninstalling the app removes the copy stored on your phone.

5. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.
